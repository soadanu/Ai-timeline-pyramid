## How the dates were handled

Several brief entries are ranges or eras, not single dates (Before 1950, 1980s, 1980s to 1990s, 2004 to 2007, 2006 to 2009, 2010s, Future). Each is kept as written for display and given a single sort value so the chronological order is stable. Where two events share a year (the Dartmouth workshop and the Logic Theorist in 1956; GPT-3 and AlphaFold 2 in 2020) the order follows the month.
